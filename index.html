New-construction style updates, without the wait.

This extensively remodeled one-and-one-half-story home delivers approximately 2,800 square feet above grade plus a fully finished lower level, creating more than 4,000 square feet across three refreshed levels.

The remodeled kitchen features updated cabinetry, new countertops, stainless-steel appliances and an open layout connected to expansive living and dining spaces. Four spacious bedrooms and four full bathrooms provide flexibility for everyday living, entertaining, guests, hobbies and working from home.

“Why wait to build a new home when everything you’ve been looking for is already here?”

The finished lower level adds an enormous recreation area, bonus room near a full bath, recessed lighting and dedicated storage. Outside, enjoy the sunroom, newer deck, large concrete patio, mature shade trees, expansive backyard and detached storage shed.
