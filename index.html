New construction without the wait.

Completely remodeled, inside and out.

This extensively remodeled 1.5-story home at 626 Lonedell Road puts 4 bedrooms and 4 full bathrooms on a private 0.40-acre lot in the heart of Arnold—about 2,800 square feet above grade, plus a fully finished lower level, for more than 4,000 square feet of finished living space across three refreshed levels.

Inside, everything feels new: all-new flooring, freshly painted walls and ceilings, updated trim, and new lighting and ceiling fans throughout. The completely remodeled kitchen brings custom cabinetry, new countertops, stainless-steel appliances and a breakfast bar into an open floorplan that flows to a separate formal dining room and the expansive main living area—anchored by a wood-burning fireplace. Hardwood floors, special millwork and built-ins run through the main level, and the master bed and bath suite adds a double vanity, separate shower and the convenience of its own laundry area.

Downstairs, the finished lower level is highlighted by an enormous recreation area with a second wood-burning fireplace, a bonus sleeping room beside a full bathroom—ideal as a guest suite or home office—recessed lighting throughout, an additional laundry space with utility sink and dual dryer hookups, and plenty of dedicated storage. Eleven rooms in all, plus a laundry chute and hookups on multiple levels.

Outdoor living is just as impressive: a bright sunroom, a newer deck, a large concrete patio, mature shade trees and an expansive fenced backyard with a detached storage shed. The oversized 2-car garage adds an epoxy-style floor, workbench and built-in storage, while new shingles and new siding give the home a fresh look from every angle. Built in 1977 and remodeled top to bottom, it delivers the space and updates of a newly built home—without the wait.

Area information & lifestyle guide

Centrally located in Arnold.

626 Lonedell Road sits in the established Dewberry Heights pocket of Arnold, Missouri, just off Jeffco Boulevard—giving buyers a quarter-acre-plus lot and a fully finished lower level while putting all three Fox C-6 schools within roughly a quarter mile, with Jeffco Boulevard shopping and dining and Arnold City Park close behind.

Well connected, close to St. Louis.

Arnold is served directly by two I-55 interchanges plus US 61 and MO 141, putting downtown St. Louis and Lambert International Airport roughly 30 minutes away. Buyers get an established residential setting without giving up quick access to the interstate.
