Freshly updated. Ready to move in.

Space that keeps on going.

This 1.5-story home at 626 Lonedell Road puts 4 bedrooms and 4 full bathrooms on a private 0.40-acre lot in the heart of Arnold—and a fully finished lower level pushes total living space toward 4,000 square feet.

Downstairs, the finished lower level adds a large recreation room, a bonus room with a full bath nearby—ideal as a fifth bedroom, guest suite or home office—a dedicated laundry area with utility sink and dual dryer hookups, and a separate storage cellar for everything else.

The big projects are already done: a new driveway, patio, walkway and deck, plus a freshly epoxy-coated floor in the attached 2-car garage. Built in 1977 and thoughtfully maintained since, this is a home that's ready for its next chapter.

Area information & lifestyle guide

Centrally located in Arnold.

626 Lonedell Road sits in an established residential pocket of Arnold, Missouri—giving buyers a quarter-acre-plus lot and a fully finished lower level while keeping Fox C-6 schools, Jeffco Boulevard shopping and dining, and Arnold City Park within easy reach.
